# Zhiji Premium UI/UX Design

## Goal

Make the existing MVP feel trustworthy enough for a premium paid consultation without adding new product scope.

## Direction

Use a high-trust consultation pattern, not a mystical luxury pattern. The page should signal human judgment, verification, privacy, and review. Avoid fake testimonials, heavy animation, new dependencies, or decorative visuals that do not support conversion.

## Changes

- Strengthen the hero with visible proof points and a more intentional chart/proof area.
- Move trust and verification language earlier in the homepage flow.
- Make pricing easier to choose by adding "best for" guidance and giving the middle tier stronger hierarchy.
- Improve paid/free intake forms with required markers, autofill, clearer reassurance, and accessible error roles.
- Refine the design system through CSS only: stronger contrast, cooler neutral surfaces, better focus states, and touch-friendly controls.

## Non-Goals

- No fake reviews or claims.
- No new account system, chart engine, or payment changes.
- No new UI library.
