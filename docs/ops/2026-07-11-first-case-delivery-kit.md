# First Case — Delivery & Feedback Kit

**For:** the founder, running the first concierge case (yourself or a close friend)
**Goal:** test the one thing that matters most — *can you produce a reading a smart person finds genuinely useful?* — and, if it lands, get an honest, de-identified first sample + real calibration data.

> This is a rehearsal that de-risks delivery. A happy friend validates the **craft**, not the **market** — a paying stranger does that. Get specific feedback, not politeness.

---

## Methodology note (read first)

Zhiji is **Chinese metaphysics**, not BaZi-only. BaZi is the recognizable anchor; draw on the **full toolkit as each case calls for it** — 八字 BaZi, 紫微斗数 Zi Wei Dou Shu, and other Chinese methods you actually practice. Two honesty rules:

1. **Only claim what you used.** In the brief, note *which* method(s) this specific reading drew on and *why each fit*. Don't imply a systematized multi-method engine that doesn't exist yet.
2. **Keep it reproducible.** For every method used, you should be able to show the calculation/rules you followed. This is what separates you from generic AI prose.

---

## Part 1 · Intake (collect before you start)

- **One decision**, concrete, roughly due within 90 days. Write it in *their* words.
- **The real facts** around it — options, constraints, deadline, what's reversible.
- **Birth data** — date, time (note if uncertain), place. Flag time-zone / true-solar-time handling.
- **Consent**, spoken or written: (a) to do the reading, (b) to a 30/90-day check-in, (c) to *maybe* use it later as a **de-identified** sample. If no to (c), that's fine — still run it.

---

## Part 2 · The written brief (this IS the 判读书 template)

Fill each block. This is the exact structure on the site's sample, so a good first case becomes our first honest sample with almost no extra work.

| Block | What goes here |
|---|---|
| **Header** | Case no. (e.g. ZJ-001) · **method(s) used** · review date · report version (v1) |
| **The question** | Their decision, in their words |
| **现实条件 / Real-world constraints** | The facts that move the answer — kept *separate* from the chart |
| **盘面观察 / Chart observation** | What the chart(s) surface, and *which method* it came from — a lens on *why* it's hard, not an instruction |
| **关键假设 / Key assumption** | What the observation rests on; when it would be wrong |
| **低后悔行动 / Low-regret next step** | Concrete facts to gather / the least-regret action, given uncertainty |
| **反例 / Where this could be wrong** | The counterexamples that would overturn it — named up front, not after the outcome |
| **边界 / Boundaries** | What this isn't (no medical/legal/immigration/investment/fertility advice; the decision stays theirs) |
| **Sign-off** | 知几案例审核 · ZJ-R01 · Method vX |

**Written and asynchronous** — no call. If they want to talk, note it as feedback (see Part 4), don't add a live session.

---

## Part 3 · The debrief (the feedback that actually matters)

Ask these *after* they've read it. Push past "nice job":

1. **Did it name something true you hadn't put into words?** Which line?
2. **Did it change what you'll actually do — or just describe you?**
3. **Honestly: would you have paid US$388 for this?** If not, what would make it worth that?
4. **What felt generic** — like it could apply to anyone?
5. **What was missing?**
6. **Was written-only enough**, or did you want to talk? (signal, not a feature to add)
7. **Name one specific person** you'd refer. (Real referral > "I'd recommend you.")

Log the answers verbatim. Especially #3 and #4 — those are the go/no-go signal.

---

## Part 4 · Turn it into proof (only if it lands, and honestly)

- **De-identify** (change identifying specifics; keep the reasoning shape).
- **Label honestly** — an early real case, *consented*, **not** a paid testimonial. Never dress a friend's free reading as an arm's-length client result.
- **Schedule the 30/90-day review** — the delayed-outcome check is the proof that actually converts strangers.
- Real public proof needs **arm's-length + delayed outcome**. One friendly case is internal go/no-go, not a homepage testimonial.

---

## What "done" looks like for case #1

You have: a filled 判读书, verbatim debrief answers (esp. would-they-pay + what-felt-generic), a consent status, and a 30/90 review on the calendar. That tells us whether the craft is real — the single most important thing to know before building anything else.
