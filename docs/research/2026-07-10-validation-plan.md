# Zhiji / 知几 — 90-Day Validation Plan

> **Superseding delivery decision — 2026-07-11:** Zhiji's initial service is fully asynchronous and written. The live/60-minute meeting, call, or session was removed. Any such references below are retained only as historical recommendations and are not current operating guidance. See the approved [asynchronous written-delivery design](../plans/2026-07-11-asynchronous-written-delivery-design.md).

## Objective

Determine whether Zhiji can become a safe, profitable premium productized practice for a narrow North American Chinese-professional segment before significant platform engineering.

Behavior beats opinion. Displayed prices, deposits, completed paid cases, delivery time, refunds, delayed reviews, referrals, and acquisition costs determine the decision.

## Definitions

**Primary ICP:** Chinese professional, roughly 28–45, in a major U.S. or Canadian metro, culturally familiar with BaZi or adjacent practices, one consequential decision due within 90 days, able to afford US$388 without financing, and willing to retain agency.

**Qualified lead:** Meets geography, decision timing, ability-to-pay, category openness, adult consent, and scope criteria; accepts disclosed software assistance; is not in acute crisis.

**Paid case:** A nonzero payment at the public cohort price, no founder-friend discount, completed service, and no reversal.

**Qualified referral:** An introduction to a named person who meets the decision-timing and category-openness criteria.

**Decision clarity:** A prespecified measure covering option definition, assumptions, controllable factors, next action, and confidence calibrated to uncertainty. Belief in “accuracy” is not the primary outcome.

## Phase 0 — Commercial Safety Gate (Days 0–10)

Before advertising for payment:

- obtain operating-location and licensing review;
- approve bilingual claims and refusal language;
- obtain accurate processor-category clearance;
- finalize scope, cancellation, deposit, refund, and complaint terms;
- implement a data map, consent matrix, retention, deletion, correction, export, and incident ownership;
- implement adults-only screening, crisis handling, restricted-topic rules, cooldowns, and incident logging;
- connect source, qualification, payment, delivery, labor, refund, and 30/90-day outcome events.

If the legal/payment/privacy gate fails, continue noncommercial interviews only.

## Phase 1 — Interviews (Days 1–21)

Conduct 40 interviews:

| Segment | Count |
|---|---:|
| U.S. primary ICP | 16 |
| Canada primary ICP | 8 |
| Singapore comparison group | 6 |
| Mainland research-only | 6 |
| Founders/executives or couples | 4 |

Record past paid behavior, live decisions, exact language, alternatives, distrust, acceptable deliverables, price evidence, and AI disclosure reactions. Do not treat “would you buy?” answers as demand.

Proceed only if North American interviews show repeated live decisions, prior relevant paid behavior, correct offer comprehension, and manageable deterministic-prediction expectations.

## Phase 2 — Message and Trust Tests (Days 12–35)

Test otherwise identical pages:

1. Private BaZi consultation / 私人八字咨询
2. Private BaZi consultation for major life transitions / 私人八字咨询（重大人生节点）
3. Structured decision reflection using BaZi / 以八字为框架的决策梳理

Primary endpoint: completed qualified intake per relevant unique visitor. Secondary endpoints: comprehension, deposit start, method/accountability view, privacy-method view, and exit reason.

Test trust modules one at a time: brand accountability and human-review provenance, calculation/calibration method, no-remedy pledge, AI disclosure, calibration-failure refund, and delayed case including a miss.

Keep referral, founder-network, community, organic, exact-search, and paid cohorts separate.

## Phase 3 — Paid Concierge Cases (Days 21–70)

Complete 30 full-price cases, at least 60% beyond the founder’s direct friend graph:

| Cohort | Cases | Public offer |
|---|---:|---|
| P1 | 10 | US$388 core; US$49 credited/refundable deposit |
| P2 | 10 | US$388 with documented workflow improvements |
| P3 | 10 | US$488 with the same stable scope |

Every case follows consent/safety, normalization, deterministic calculation, historical calibration, concrete decision intake, a bilingual brief carrying case/reviewer-role/method provenance, a 60-minute session, bounded clarification, 30/90-day review, and data-control workflow. The actual internal reviewer identity must remain auditable even though it is not public.

Log minutes separately for intake, normalization, calibration, interpretation, drafting, editing, translation, consultation, clarification, reviews, admin, refunds, and complaints.

## Phase 4 — Outcomes (Days 45–90+)

At delivery, 30 days, and 90 days measure:

- decision clarity and agency;
- calibrated confidence and ability to name uncertainty;
- action taken and use of appropriate domain professionals;
- regret, distress, repeated-reading urge, or perceived pressure;
- satisfaction with practitioner, report, disclosure, privacy, and price;
- actual qualified referral;
- interest in an annual review after a new trigger.

Do not use outcome review as an upsell. True 90-day outcomes for later cases will extend beyond day 90; issue an interim and final cohort readout separately.

## Decision Thresholds

These are founder decision rules, not industry benchmarks.

| Metric | Success | Caution | Kill / redesign |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Visitor → lead, n≥2,500 | ≥8% | 3–7.9% | <3% |
| Qualified lead rate, n≥150 intakes | ≥50% | 25–49% | <25% |
| Qualified lead → deposit, n≥100 | ≥20% | 8–19% | <8% |
| Deposit → core | ≥45% | 20–44% | <20% |
| US$388 standardized price acceptance | ≥50% | 25–49% | <25% |
| US$488 directional acceptance | ≥35% | 15–34% | <15% |
| Refund + chargeback | ≤5% | 6–10% | >10% |
| Median total delivery labor | ≤3.5h | 3.6–5h | >5h |
| US$488 target pre-CAC margin | ≥54% | 40–53.9% | <40% |
| Fully loaded CAC | ≤25% of first revenue | 26–40% | >40% |
| CSAT | ≥4.6/5 | 4.2–4.59 | <4.2 |
| Actual qualified referral | ≥20% | 8–19% | <8% |
| 30-day participation | ≥70% | 45–69% | <45% |
| 90-day participation | ≥50% | 25–49% | <25% |
| Practitioner capacity | ≥6 cases/35h week | 4–5 | <4 |
| Critical safety failure | 0 | One contained near miss | Any uncontained or repeated breach |

Always report denominators and uncertainty.

## Engineering Gates

| Capability | Evidence gate |
|---|---|
| Automated deterministic charting | 30 paid cases stabilize the method and show material normalization cost |
| Accounts/dashboard | At least 20 clients independently need repeated history access |
| Longitudinal profile | At least 50% 90-day participation, explicit consent, and demonstrated utility |
| Subscription | 100 paid cases and healthy repeat tied to new triggers without dependency signals |
| Native app | Proven repeated value cannot be served well on web and platform review is cleared |
| Multiple practitioners/marketplace | Demand exceeds capacity and at least two practitioners pass blinded QA |
| Complex AI/fine-tuning | Repeated task has measured cost/error, privacy clearance, consented data, and substantive human review |

## Final Outcomes

- **Build Model B:** Legal gates clear, 30 cases complete, price/delivery/economics meet thresholds, one channel works, delayed outcomes are healthy.
- **Remain boutique:** Customers pay and refer but trust or quality remains founder-bound.
- **Reposition:** Category interest exists but premium price or decision language fails.
- **Stop:** Viability depends on fear/determinism, unsafe repeat use, failed legal/payment gates, weak conversion, or uneconomic delivery.
