# 90-Day GTM Plan — Zhiji Founding Cohort

*Created: 2026-07-24*
*ICP: Category-aware Chinese professionals, 28–45, North America, one consequential decision within 90 days*
*Goal: 30 completed full-price cases, 60%+ beyond founder's direct friend graph*

---

## Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1–3)

### Objective: Build waitlist to 200+ emails, validate messaging

**Week 1 — Infrastructure**
- [ ] Deploy tracking (GA4 or PostHog) — set `NEXT_PUBLIC_GA_ID`
- [ ] Connect waitlist form to backend (Supabase `subscribers` table or Mailchimp)
- [ ] Set up UTM convention (see `docs/plans/2026-07-24-tracking-plan.md`)
- [ ] Create 3 landing page variants for A/B testing (headline A/B/C from CRO audit)
- [ ] Set up WeChat Official Account or Xiaohongshu business account

**Week 2 — Content seeding**
- [ ] Write 3 long-form posts for Xiaohongshu / WeChat:
  1. "两份 offer 怎么选？一个命理师不会告诉你的事" (decision framework, not prediction)
  2. "为什么我不卖转运物" (no-remedy positioning, trust builder)
  3. "八字排盘为什么三个师傅三个结果" (calculation transparency, differentiator)
- [ ] Write 1 LinkedIn post (English): "Why I built a BaZi consultation that refuses to predict your future"
- [ ] Create 1 sample reading excerpt as a downloadable PDF (lead magnet)

**Week 3 — Warm outreach**
- [ ] Personal outreach to 30 people in founder's network who match ICP
- [ ] Ask 10 of them for 20-min discovery calls (validation interviews)
- [ ] Share waitlist link with 5 Chinese professional communities (WeChat groups, Discord servers)
- [ ] Post in 3 relevant subreddits (r/ChineseLanguage, r/immigration, r/careerguidance) — value-first, no pitch

**KPIs by end of Phase 1:**
- Waitlist: 200+ emails
- Discovery calls: 10+ completed
- Content published: 4+ pieces
- Site visits: 500+

## Phase 2: Amplification (Weeks 4–7)

### Objective: Scale waitlist to 800+, build social proof pipeline

**Channel strategy:**

| Channel | Cadence | Content type | Target |
|---------|---------|-------------|--------|
| Xiaohongshu | 3x/week | Decision-framework posts, chart education, behind-the-scenes | Chinese diaspora 28–40 |
| WeChat Official Account | 1x/week | Long-form essays, sample reading excerpts | Professionals 30–45 |
| LinkedIn | 2x/week | English posts on decision-making + cultural frameworks | Bilingual professionals |
| WeChat groups | Ongoing | Value comments, not pitches | Existing communities |
| Podcast guesting | 2 appearances | Chinese diaspora / career / immigration podcasts | Warm audiences |

**Content pillars (rotate weekly):**
1. **Decision clarity** — "How to think about a hard choice" (universal, not mystical)
2. **Method transparency** — "What BaZi actually calculates" (trust builder)
3. **Anti-pattern** — "What bad practitioners do" (differentiator)
4. **Client voice** — Anonymized decision stories (social proof, with consent)

**Paid experiments (budget: $500 total):**
- $200 Xiaohongshu promoted posts (top 2 performing organic posts)
- $200 WeChat Moments ads (targeting: Chinese language, 28–45, US/CA)
- $100 LinkedIn sponsored post (English, targeting: Chinese surname + professional title)

**KPIs by end of Phase 2:**
- Waitlist: 800+ emails
- Site visits: 2,500+
- Discovery calls: 30+ completed
- Content engagement: 50+ comments/shares per Xiaohongshu post
- Email open rate: 40%+

## Phase 3: Conversion (Weeks 8–12)

### Objective: Open founding cohort, convert 30 paid cases

**Week 8 — Pre-launch sequence**
- Day 1: Email to waitlist — "What we learned from 30 discovery calls" (value + anticipation)
- Day 3: Email — Sample reading excerpt (full PDF, not gated)
- Day 5: Email — "How the process works" (process transparency, objection handling)
- Day 7: Email — "Founding cohort opens in 48 hours" (scarcity + timing)

**Week 9 — Launch week**
- Day 1: Open 12 founding seats. Email to waitlist with application link.
- Day 1: Xiaohongshu post — "首批 12 席，今天开放" (scarcity + social proof)
- Day 3: WeChat post — First application received (anonymized, with permission)
- Day 5: LinkedIn post — "We opened 12 seats. Here's what happened." (transparency)
- Day 7: If seats remain, extend by 48 hours with email to waitlist

**Weeks 10–12 — Delivery + review cycle**
- Deliver first 5 cases within 7 business days of acceptance
- Request anonymized feedback from each delivered case
- Write 1 case study per delivered case (with consent, anonymized)
- Open second wave of 12 seats if first wave sold out
- Begin 30-day written review cycle for first delivered cases

**KPIs by end of Phase 3:**
- Paid cases: 30 completed
- Revenue: $11,640 (30 × $388)
- Waitlist conversion rate: 3.75% (30/800)
- Delivery time: <7 business days median
- Refund rate: <10%
- 30-day review participation: 60%+
- NPS or satisfaction score: 8+/10

## Risk Register

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Waitlist doesn't convert to paid | Discovery calls in Phase 1 validate willingness to pay before scaling |
| Xiaohongshu content gets flagged | No prediction claims, no fear language, no remedy mentions |
| Delivery takes too long | Cap at 12 cases per wave; don't open wave 2 until wave 1 delivered |
| No public practitioner identity hurts trust | Compensate with process transparency, sample quality, and case provenance |
| Legal/processor gates not ready | Payments stay closed until Supabase + webhook + legal review complete |
