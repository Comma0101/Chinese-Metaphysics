# Zhiji Brand Accountability Design

**Status:** Approved  
**Decision date:** 2026-07-10

## Decision

Zhiji will not use a practitioner's real name, personal photograph, or public
personal biography. Public accountability belongs to the Zhiji brand and its
documented service process, not to a personality-led "master" brand.

This replaces the previous product assumption that the founder would be a
publicly named practitioner.

## Public identity

- Public brand: **知几 / Zhiji**.
- Product category: a private, human-reviewed Chinese metaphysics consultation
  for one consequential decision.
- Do not publish a real name, portrait, personal lineage, or personal case-count
  claim.
- Do not invent a practitioner persona, pseudonymous biography, credential,
  school, title, or photograph.
- Do not use `具名咨询师`, `named practitioner`, `master`, or equivalent personal
  authority language as a Zhiji proof point.

The seller's lawful identity is a separate compliance matter. Payment
processors, banks, tax authorities, counsel, insurers, and customer contracts
receive the entity or merchant information required by law and policy. This
does not make the founder's identity a public marketing asset.

## Accountability model

Every delivered case must identify the work without identifying a private
individual:

- Zhiji case number;
- reviewer role or stable internal reviewer ID;
- calculation and method version;
- review and delivery timestamps;
- report version and correction history;
- scope, assumptions, uncertainties, and excluded questions;
- a support, complaint, correction, export, and deletion route.

Recommended report sign-off:

> 知几案例审核 · ZJ-R01 · Method v1.0  
> Human-reviewed by Zhiji · Case ID · Review date

`ZJ-R01` is an operational identifier, not a fabricated person or credential.

## Human and software disclosure

The service may say `由知几人工审核 / Human-reviewed by Zhiji` only when a
person has substantively reviewed the chart inputs, interpretation, decision
brief, and final corrections.

Software may assist with deterministic calculation, organization, translation,
formatting, consistency checks, scheduling, and follow-up. It may not be
described as an autonomous master, and its draft must not be presented as human
review without substantive review.

Public disclosure should describe tasks rather than identity:

> Software assists with calculation support, organization, translation, and
> consistency checks. Zhiji completes a substantive human review before any
> interpretation is delivered.

## Trust architecture

Because Zhiji will not borrow trust from a public personality, the product must
earn trust through observable controls:

1. an inspectable method specification and calculation trace;
2. separate labels for facts, chart observations, interpretations, and actions;
3. explicit uncertainty, method limits, disagreements, and corrections;
4. complete fictional samples showing the actual paid artifact;
5. fixed scope, price, delivery, cancellation, and refund terms;
6. a no-fear, no-remedy, no-affiliate, no-dependency policy;
7. concrete privacy, retention, export, correction, and deletion controls;
8. delayed 30/90-day review that measures utility, agency, and harm rather than
   advertising an accuracy rate.

Anonymous delivery creates a larger cold-start trust burden. US$388 remains a
validation hypothesis and must not be justified through invented authority.

## Site and artifact changes

- Replace the practitioner/About section with **Method & Accountability / 方法与责任**.
- Replace personal-signature and portrait concepts with a case-review colophon,
  case number, method version, and report revision record.
- Replace `主理人会联系你` with `知几案例团队会联系你` or a precise service action.
- Replace payment gates requiring a practitioner profile with gates requiring
  method disclosure, service terms, privacy terms, merchant readiness, and an
  acceptance/refund rule.
- Preserve competitor research showing that named practitioners command trust;
  relabel it as a strategic disadvantage Zhiji must validate, not a Zhiji
  feature.
- Preserve historical design documents as history. Add superseding notes where
  their named-person assumptions could mislead future implementation.

## Method architecture

Customers buy one decision case, not a count of metaphysical systems. BaZi is
the default structural framework; Zi Wei Dou Shu may be included when birth-time
confidence and the question make it useful. The report must keep observations
from each method separate before showing convergence, complement, disagreement,
or insufficient evidence.

The exact BaZi and Zi Wei schools and calculation rules remain unapproved until
the internal method specification is written. Zhiji must not claim lineage or
canonical authority that it cannot substantiate.

## Non-goals

- no public founder profile or portrait;
- no fabricated pseudonymous master;
- no personal credential theater;
- no autonomous AI reader;
- no attempt to compensate for anonymity with mystical certainty;
- no additional divination systems in v1;
- no public paid launch until legal, payment, privacy, method, delivery, and
  database integration gates are cleared.

## Acceptance criteria

- Canonical product context no longer says the founder is publicly named.
- Current public pages make no promise of a real name, portrait, or public
  practitioner biography.
- Human-review language is task-specific and operationally truthful.
- Report samples use brand review provenance and method versions.
- Launch gates require lawful merchant identity and operational controls, not a
  public personal identity.
- Tests fail if public copy reintroduces named-practitioner or fake-authority
  claims.
