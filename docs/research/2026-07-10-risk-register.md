# Zhiji / 知几 — Critical Risk Register

**Research cut-off:** 2026-07-10  
**Status:** Strategic research, not legal advice. Recheck live sources and obtain qualified specialist review for actual operating facts.

## Launch Posture

- Web-first, one-off, adults-only, human-led, and disclosed-software-assisted.
- Deterministic chart engine; no autonomous emotional companion.
- Conditional North American pilot after location, licensing, claims, payments, privacy, tax, and safety review.
- No mainland launch, subscription, mobile app, marketplace, or public AI master.

## Critical Risks

| Risk | Severity | Required mitigation | Product consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Local fortune-telling restrictions or licensing conflict | High | Counsel reviews entity, practitioner, location, remote delivery, claims, and local rules | No paid launch until cleared; rebranding does not change substance |
| Processor termination or reserve | High | Describe the actual service and obtain account-specific approval; maintain backup and dispute monitoring | Processor clearance is a pre-payment gate |
| Fear, certainty, scientific, accuracy, or guaranteed claims | High | Approved bilingual claims, scripts, evidence file, and complaint review | Sell a cultural interpretive framework, not prediction or science |
| Unauthorized medical, mental-health, legal, immigration, investment, fertility, or relationship directives | Critical | Contextual refusals, practitioner training, appropriate professional referrals, QA | Intake must detect restricted topics; a footer is insufficient |
| Sensitive life-history breach or AI-vendor reuse | Critical | Data minimization, pseudonymous case IDs, MFA/RBAC, encryption, logs, vendor controls, no training by default | Privacy architecture is part of premium quality |
| Inadequate consent, rights, retention, correction, deletion, or transfers | High | Purpose-by-purpose notices and choices; localized review; operational rights workflow | Do not launch with a generic policy or JSON file store |
| Calculation, time-zone, translation, or hallucination error | High | Deterministic engine, versioned rules/TZ database, golden fixtures, provenance, human sign-off | LLM never calculates the canonical chart |
| Misleading human-authorship or hidden AI | High | Disclose actual tasks and vendors before processing; log substantive human review and the actual internal reviewer | Every interpretation carries a stable reviewer-role ID, method version, review date, and correction history |

## Minimum Privacy and Safety Controls

1. Collect only what is required for the specific case.
2. Separate service, AI processing, recording, marketing, testimonial, research, and training choices.
3. Default model training to off.
4. Keep payment data with the processor and identity separate from case narrative where practical.
5. Encrypt data, require MFA, restrict case access, log changes, and prepare an incident plan.
6. Corrected birth data invalidate dependent outputs and create a visible version history.
7. Implement access, correction, export, and deletion rather than merely promising them in copy.
8. Refuse prohibited professional-advice and crisis topics contextually.
9. Add a cooling-off rule for repeated questions about the same crisis.
10. Keep a safety, complaint, correction, refund, and refusal log.

## Current Codebase Implications

The current synchronous JSON store, success-page payment confirmation, absent deletion/export workflow, and absent transactional delivery/review operations are not sufficient for live sensitive cases. These are launch blockers even during concierge validation; they are not speculative platform features.

## Specialist Review Triggers

Obtain specialist advice before paid operation in any locality with category-specific rules; any mainland, EEA, or Quebec activity; subscriptions or nonrefundable deposits; native apps; persistent chat/memory/personas; AI training on cases; recordings or voice analysis; minors/families; health/legal/financial positioning; multiple practitioners; testimonial incentives; or processor uncertainty.
