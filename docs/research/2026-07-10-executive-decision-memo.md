# Zhiji / 知几 — Executive Decision Memo

**Decision date:** 2026-07-10  
**Research cut-off:** 2026-07-10  
**Recommendation:** Conditional go for a premium, human-accountable productized practice. No-go for a consumer platform or mainland launch now.

> **Superseding decision — 2026-07-10:** Zhiji will not publish a practitioner's real name or photograph. References below to named-practitioner accountability describe the earlier recommendation or competitor evidence. Current Zhiji accountability follows the approved [brand-accountability design](../plans/2026-07-10-brand-accountability-design.md).

## Decision

The long-term goal remains a full product, but “full product” means a complete premium case experience and the secure operating system behind it. It does not initially mean a native app, subscription, autonomous AI reader, public community, or marketplace.

Fund a 90-day validation sprint centered on 30 completed paid cases before significant platform engineering. Build only the safety-, privacy-, payment-, and delivery-critical infrastructure required to run those cases responsibly.

## Category

Use a recognizable shelf:

> **Private BaZi consultation for major life transitions**  
> **私人八字咨询（重大人生节点）**

Keep BaZi visible. Use decision language to explain the outcome: timing, recurring patterns, risk posture, options, assumptions, and boundaries. “Decision advisory” by itself is too unfamiliar and risks comparison with credentialed professional coaching or regulated advice.

## Initial Customer

The primary ICP is a category-aware or culturally close Chinese professional, roughly 28–45, in North America, with one consequential decision due within 90 days. Typical triggers include:

- choosing between offers or career paths;
- staying overseas, returning, or relocating;
- a founder or leadership transition;
- a serious relationship or family decision;
- reconciling private preferences with family and cultural expectations.

The customer can spend US$388 without financial distress, prefers Mandarin or bilingual delivery for intimate questions, and dislikes fear and remedy sales. Whether this customer will trust a brand-accountable service without a public practitioner identity is now a primary validation question.

“Educated skeptic” is a useful psychographic but not an ICP. Skepticism adds a trust burden; it does not create urgency.

## Brand Accountability

Zhiji will not publish a practitioner's real name or photograph and will not invent a master persona. Before launch, the product needs a public method-and-accountability disclosure stating:

- what receives substantive human review;
- the method and calculation versions used;
- how case IDs and stable reviewer-role IDs work;
- how software and AI assist;
- how corrections and report versions are recorded;
- professional boundaries, complaint routes, and referral rules;
- how the lawful seller and actual internal reviewer remain accountable where contracts, processors, security, or law require them.

Do not invent or inflate a persona, lineage, certification, scientific authority, or regulated-advisor status.

## First Complete Paid Experience

1. Consent, scope, privacy, crisis, third-party-data, retention, correction, deletion, and refund terms.
2. Birth location, time-zone, DST, and method normalization with uncertainty handling.
3. A US$49 calibration deposit credited to the full service or refunded under a predefined process gate.
4. Historical calibration recorded with hits, misses, ambiguity, and revisions.
5. One concrete decision intake with deadline, facts, constraints, and professional-advice boundaries.
6. A human-reviewed bilingual brief separating chart interpretation, real-world facts, assumptions, uncertainty, scenarios, and action boundaries.
7. A 60-minute private consultation delivered under Zhiji's documented human-review process.
8. Fourteen days of bounded clarification.
9. Structured 30/90-day reviews.
10. Exportable, correctable, deletable, versioned records.

The deterministic chart engine must not be an LLM. Calculation method, time-zone database, true-solar-time choices, uncertain birth-time treatment, and version must be reproducible.

## Pricing Hypotheses

| Offer | Test price | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Calibration deposit | US$49 | Credited/refunded process gate; not a standalone interpretation |
| Founding core | US$388 | First 20 cases |
| Target core | US$488 | Test after workflow and trust proof |
| Deep case | US$688–788 | Later, bounded multi-scenario work |
| Consented couple case | About US$888 | Only after separate consent and harm controls |
| Annual review | US$188–288 | Only after evidence of healthy repeat value |

Do not launch a subscription. Serious decisions are episodic, and recurring anxiety must not become the retention mechanism.

The current US$49/99/249 tiers are MVP hypotheses and conflict with the researched premium workflow. US$249 is unlikely to support three or more hours of accountable human work plus acquisition, refunds, administration, and secure operations.

## Economics Hypothesis

At US$388, the supplied model estimates roughly US$159 pre-CAC gross profit under a 5% refund allowance, 2.9% + US$0.30 payment cost, US$7 software cost, 3.25 practitioner hours at US$55/hour, and 0.5 admin hour at US$25/hour. That is about 43% of net sales.

At US$488 with the same work, modeled pre-CAC gross profit is about US$251, or 54% of net sales. These values are not evidence; replace them with case-level time, refund, and acquisition logs.

## Go-to-Market

- Start with warm referrals and Chinese diaspora professional communities.
- Use the U.S. West Coast as an acquisition hypothesis, not a legal operating conclusion.
- Add Toronto and Vancouver after initial case proof.
- Use XiaoHongShu for language and discovery tests, WeChat/email for nurture and referrals, and tightly bounded exact-intent search for demand capture.
- Keep referral, founder-network, direct outreach, organic, and paid cohorts separate.

Content thesis:

> **一件真实决定 + 一条可复核的推理链 + 一个30/90天复盘**  
> One real decision + one inspectable reasoning chain + one 30/90-day review.

Publish misses and ambiguity, not only hits.

## Hard Gates

Before accepting payment, clear operating location, licensing, claims, processor underwriting, privacy, terms, refunds, safety, and crisis handling. Research flagged a potentially material Los Angeles legal issue; only qualified counsel can resolve the actual service and operating facts.

Validation success requires evidence across full-price conversion, delivery time, refunds, gross margin, acquisition, delayed participation, referrals, and safety. Market enthusiasm, social engagement, or interview approval is insufficient.

## What Not to Build Yet

- customer dashboard or broad account system;
- subscription or daily horoscope;
- generalized AI chatbot or “master” persona;
- public feed, streak, luck score, countdown, or repeated-answer loop;
- mobile app;
- practitioner marketplace or multi-practitioner system;
- mainland distribution;
- health, immigration, legal, investment, fertility, or deterministic relationship advice.

## Brutal Verdict

Zhiji is currently a plausible premium practice that may become a productized human-plus-software service. It is not yet a proven venture-scale platform.

The largest failure risk is that culturally aligned affluent professionals admire the concept but will not trust or pay a premium anonymous brand often enough at an economical CAC. The strongest possible advantage is a consented, versioned calibration-and-outcome ledger tied to deterministic calculation, substantive human review, and reviewer QA. Before hundreds of cases, the actual moat is process quality, useful outcomes, privacy, and referrals—not personal authority.

Proceed only through evidence gates. The end goal is still a full product; the research changes the sequence, not the ambition.
