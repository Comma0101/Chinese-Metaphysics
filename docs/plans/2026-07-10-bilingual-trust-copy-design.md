# Zhiji Bilingual Trust Copy Design

**Date:** 2026-07-10  
**Status:** Approved  
**Source of truth:** `.agents/product-marketing.md` and `docs/research/`

> **Superseded in part — 2026-07-10:** The public named-practitioner biography and photograph requirement in this document is replaced by the approved [brand-accountability design](2026-07-10-brand-accountability-design.md). Zhiji now uses substantive human review, case/reviewer-role provenance, method versions, and correction history without a public personal identity. The customer-language and anti-scam copy principles below remain active.

> **Superseded delivery decision — 2026-07-11:** Zhiji's initial product no longer includes a live or 60-minute consultation. Delivery is asynchronous: a human-reviewed written decision brief, one consolidated written follow-up, and written 30/90-day reviews. References below to a 60-minute session or "one private consultation" as a synchronous meeting are preserved as historical design recommendations and are not current product requirements.

## Objective

Make Zhiji read like a serious private practice for a specific decision—not a
fortune-telling funnel, an anonymous AI report, or a startup manufacturing
scarcity. Chinese and English must carry the same facts while sounding native
in each language.

## Positioning decision

Use a practitioner-led private-practice posture with a decision-led opening.
Keep the recognizable category visible:

> 私人八字咨询（重大人生节点）  
> Private BaZi consultation for major life transitions

The homepage leads with the customer's immediate situation and the concrete
service. Method, boundaries, pilot mechanics, and price follow only after the
visitor understands what they receive.

## Why the current copy fails

- It opens with a metaphysical slogan instead of a recognizable service.
- It repeats `founding pilot`, invitation, calibration, fit, and deposit before
  showing the practitioner or deliverable.
- It uses defensive negation so often that it primes visitors to expect abuse.
- `calibration`, `fit`, and `did the call hold` imply quasi-scientific precision
  while remaining unclear.
- The illustrative sample makes precise future-timing recommendations without a
  defensible method note.
- English mirrors Chinese syntax and contains unnatural service language.
- `one named person` is not trust evidence. A public name and substantiated
  biography remain a launch requirement.

## Page argument

### 1. Above the fold

Lead with one concrete promise: a private BaZi consultation for one real
decision. Immediately identify the audience, decision types, written brief,
private consultation, and client agency.

Primary CTA explains the service before asking for commitment. The invitation
application is secondary.

### 2. Situations served

Mirror customer language: two offers, stay or return, relocate, founder role,
relationship, or family decision. Avoid generic `unlock your potential` and
abstract personality claims.

### 3. What the client receives

Name observable artifacts:

- birth-time/location and method check after acceptance;
- a written decision brief;
- one private consultation with the practitioner;
- bounded clarification;
- 30/90-day review.

Do not sell page count, AI personalization, or mystical authority.

### 4. Practitioner

The practitioner section must eventually contain public name, real photograph,
actual training/method, case experience, personal responsibilities, software
role, and professional boundaries. Until those facts are supplied, copy must
not imply credentials, lineage, scientific authority, or an established case
record.

### 5. Illustrative sample

Replace the current synthetic prediction with a fictional decision-brief
walkthrough. Separate:

- question and real-world constraints;
- chart observation;
- interpretive assumption;
- option trade-offs;
- low-regret next step;
- what would change or invalidate the view.

The sample remains explicitly fictional. It must not claim a real client,
consent, accuracy, or validated timing.

### 6. Method and boundaries

Explain BaZi once, plainly, as a traditional interpretive framework. Explain
historical review as a check on data and interpretive usefulness—not proof.
Use one compact no-remedy/no-guarantee/professional-advice boundary block rather
than repeating defensive claims throughout the page.

### 7. Process and price

Move pilot language below the service explanation. Publicly describe US$49 as
`受理与校准定金 / case assessment deposit`; state that it is credited to the
US$388 founding engagement or returned only under the written process rule.
Never use fake urgency, countdowns, `exclusive`, or vague scarcity.

## Bilingual voice

### Chinese

- Contemporary, calm Mandarin.
- Short, specific sentences.
- Use category language customers recognize: 私人八字咨询, offer, 去留, 迁移,
  判断依据, 假设与不确定性, 复盘.
- Reserve the poetic line `命是结构，不是结局` as a small brand motto, not the
  sales headline.
- Avoid legalistic repetition and pseudo-classical mysticism.

### English

- Write independently as a discreet private professional service.
- Prefer `written brief`, `private consultation`, `second perspective`,
  `assumptions and limits`, and `the decision remains yours`.
- Avoid `fate`, `judgment`, `guru`, `the call held`, `named human`, and literal
  translations of Chinese rhetorical structures.

## Trust rules

Every public claim must be one of:

1. an observable deliverable;
2. a current operational policy;
3. a clearly labeled hypothesis or pilot term;
4. an honest limitation.

Do not invent testimonials, client counts, accuracy rates, credentials, or
outcomes. Do not treat the illustrative chart as proof. Keep the practitioner
identity gate visible in launch documentation until real biography facts exist.

## Conversion hierarchy

1. `查看服务内容 / See what is included`
2. `已有邀请，开始申请 / Have an invitation? Apply`
3. `查看演示判读 / View the illustrative brief`

The public homepage must not send an uninvited visitor directly into a form
that requires a secret code without first explaining that limitation.

## Success criteria

- A visitor can identify audience, category, deliverable, price path, and next
  step within the first screen and first two sections.
- Chinese reads as native contemporary service copy.
- English reads as original English, not translation.
- The homepage contains no unsubstantiated practitioner or outcome claim.
- The sample contains no deterministic recommendation or unsupported timing
  prediction.
- Pilot and legal mechanics support the offer instead of dominating it.
